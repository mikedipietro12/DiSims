Ion spread check

Runs every live beaker in the Guide for 12 s, then samples where the dissolved ions sit in the zoom window for 30 s. Only ions inside the window are counted (ions also drift past its edge, out of view). Height 0 is the solid, 1 the top of the window. A row is healthy when its mean height is at or above the even-spread mean and its bottom-quarter share is at or below the even-spread share (the window is round, so even is not 0.5). Serve the repo (e.g. python3 -m http.server) or open with file access allowed.
