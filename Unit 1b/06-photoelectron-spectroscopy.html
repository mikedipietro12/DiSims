      What the readout shows

      The graph under the instrument is a photoelectron spectrum. The horizontal axis is binding energy (ionization energy) in MJ/mol — how tightly each electron was held. The vertical axis is peak height — how many electrons arrived at that energy.

      Electrons from this atom only appear at two places on the axis: 0.85 and 4.25 MJ/mol. Nothing in between. Those are quantized energy levels — discrete allowed energies, the same idea as the levels on an energy-level diagram (and on the Bohr-style diagram you may have drawn on paper), without needing orbits.

      Peak height tracks how many electrons share each level: 3 at the shallower (valence) energy, 2 at the deeper (core) one. Position tells you the energy; height tells you the count.
