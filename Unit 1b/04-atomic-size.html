      Module 04 · Act 1

      Atomic radius

      The atomic radius is the distance from the nucleus out to the furthest electron. For carbon, that means the valence shell — the electrons outside the core.

      Before we move on, we are going to practice creating a hypothesis.

      Create a hypothesis for these two questions:

      1. How will the radius of the atoms change moving left to right in a row on the periodic table?
      2. How will the radius of the atoms change moving down the periodic table?

      Make sure you are thorough in your reasoning.

      Write them down and check as you go. Be prepared to share at the end of class.

      The radius decreases moving left to right on the periodic table; does this match your hypothesis? Why might this be the case?

      Look what happens as we move down the group, how would you describe this change?

      Start with oxygen, and move down the periodic table to see how radius changes

      Now that we know what happens when we move across and down the periodic table, could you hypothesize what happens when we make IONS from atoms?

      Write it down and check as you go. Be prepared to share.
