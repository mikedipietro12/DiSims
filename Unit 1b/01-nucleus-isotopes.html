      1 · Ionisation

      The sample is vaporised. An electron beam knocks electrons off atoms → positive ions.

      2 · Acceleration

      Electric plates pull the ions into a fast beam through a slit.

      3 · Deflection

      A magnetic field bends the beam. Lighter ions curve more sharply.

      4 · Detection

      Ions hit the collector. The chart recorder prints a spectrum — peak height = abundance.
