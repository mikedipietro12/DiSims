# Design State: Solubility & Ksp (AP Chem 12)

_Last updated: 2026-11-17 by writing-design-plans (strategy approved, plan drafted)_

## Brief
- **Problem:** Students know K, Q and Le Châtelier but meet solubility as new vocabulary; g/L ↔ mol/L ↔ Ksp conversions trip them up, and Ksp / Q vs Ksp / common ion become rules to memorise instead of one equilibrium picture
- **Primary persona:** AP Chem 12 student after the equilibrium unit; 11H solutions material needs a refresher. Secondary: the teacher presenting the Guide as the lesson
- **Brief document:** `docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-11-17-solubility.md`
- **Discovery notes:** `DISCOVERY.md`

## Design Principles
1. Same K, now for a solid
2. Every number has a picture
3. The Guide explains, the rooms measure
4. Rehearse the method, keep the labs' secrets
5. Readable from the back row, and without colour

## Hard Rules (from discovery)
1. **No kidney-stone chemistry in any room** (no calcium oxalate / phosphate, urine pH, citrate, diet). The Kidney Stones Lab stays a mystery
2. **Don't copy the class labs.** Qual Lab uses Pb²⁺ / Sr²⁺ / Mg²⁺ (real lab uses Ag⁺ / Ba²⁺ / Fe³⁺)
3. **No "AI-tell" eyebrow labels** above headings (repo `AGENTS.md`)
4. No em dashes in student copy · no personified particles · no dot-grid backgrounds · robust, interruptible animation (no reduced-motion branches)
5. **Calculations as unit analysis** (repo `AGENTS.md`): every non-K calculation is a chain of stacked fractions with units, cancelled units struck through. No `n = m ÷ M`, `c = n ÷ V` or `c₁V₁ = c₂V₂`

## Taste Profile
- **Direction:** Toy Box (inherited) + **Charge colours** (A): cations rose `#f06a84`, anions amber `#ffd257`, every ion labelled with symbol + charge; real precipitate colours; solids always faceted crystals
- **Tokens:** `assets/sol.css`
- **Taste document:** `docs/designpowers/taste/2026-11-17-solubility-taste.md`
- **Type:** Space Grotesk + Space Mono
- **Charge convention:** red = + / yellow = −, now extended to ions (rose / amber)
