The chosen look (direction A) with the teacher's changes, in four scenes: 0.10 M HCl beside 0.10 M HF (numbers from the Data Booklet), NH₃ taking a proton from water, the Kw balance as acid and then base is added, and a phenolphthalein endpoint. Directions B and C are retired; C's night-lab stage is kept as the dark-mode look.

Use the moon button (top right) for dark mode. Tap any scene to restart it.

What this look includes

  • Light mode: ink and spheres. Dark mode: the night-lab stage (dark navy, glowing atoms, rim-lit glass).
  • H₃O⁺ is red #d62828 site-wide (was magenta), so pink only ever means phenolphthalein. OH⁻ violet-blue, other anions yellow, other cations rose.
  • Particles show their atoms: H₃O⁺ is O + 3 H, OH⁻ is O + H; the role colour sits on the oxygen, hydrogens stay white.
  • Proton transfers switch between Lewis, ball-and-stick and space-filling. Ball-and-stick uses role colours (molecules grey, OH⁻ violet-blue, NH₄⁺ rose). Space-filling uses the classic atom colours (O red, N blue, H white) with a charge haze around each ion: red for +, yellow for −, the site's δ+ / δ− colours. The moving H keeps a red ring in every model.
  • Kw bars: rounded, gradient fill, no ink edge.
