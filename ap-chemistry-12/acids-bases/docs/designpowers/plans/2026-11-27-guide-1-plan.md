## Stage frame: visual primary (teacher, 2026-11-27)
Badge at the top; tabs and the model switch where a section has them; **the picture, as big as the stage allows**;
the current equation or calculation under it, large; question cards where a section asks one; a fixed footer with
Back / Next step (bottom right). **No sentences on the stage**: no step text, no "each step appears here", no captions
or how-to lines, no full-explanation toggle. Next step drives the picture even when no text appears. Anything that
needs explaining goes in the section text beside the stage. Card reasons show in reading view only. Applies to every
Guide in this unit (and the repo memory `presenting-tool`).
