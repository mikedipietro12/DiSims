Task 0 · design-taste

Paper inside the toy box

Three ways to draw Lewis structures inside the Toy Box world. Each one shows the same four test cases: CO₂ (double bonds), NH₄⁺ (brackets and charge), CO (formal charge), and an H₂O that is still missing a lone pair (how a count that is off looks). Real paper, graph-paper and dot-grid textures are out, so the "paper" is the plain white tray.

Direction A

Ink on the tray

Chosen 2026-11-10

Classic textbook Lewis: bold ink letters, ink bonds and ink dots, drawn straight on a plain white tray. It looks just like what students write on a test. No colour at all while drawing Lewis structures. Atom colour arrives later, with VSEPR and 3D.

  • +Closest to tests and the board
  • +Calm at projector scale
  • −Least "toy"; atoms feel like text
Direction B

Vinyl chips

Each atom is a small matte model-kit sphere with its symbol on it. Bonds are ink rods, and lone pairs are IMF's gold Lewis dots, so the electrons students draw here are the same ones that become IMF's lone-pair lobes.

  • +Strongest IMF hand-off
  • +Big, obvious touch targets
  • −Gold lone pairs vs ink bonding pairs: two looks for electrons
Direction C

Keycap tiles

Atoms are chunky keycap tiles, matching the Toy Box buttons, with a stripe in the atom's kit colour. Bonds are thick ink bars, and lone pairs are ink dots.

  • +Most tactile and game-like
  • +Chunky, obvious tap targets
  • −Square tiles hide the angles that VSEPR needs later
Locked rule

Formal charge: teaching, then practice

In the first formal-charge beats, the full equation is written out for every atom with the numbers filled in. From the practice beats on, only the compact pill is shown (and only when it is not zero).

Shared in every direction (proposed)

  • Formal charge: a small ink-outlined pill in Space Mono (−1, +1). It is drawn only when non-zero and never uses red or yellow, because formal charge is bookkeeping, not a real δ charge.
  • Count that is off: a dashed ink ring around the atom plus a mono tag (6 / 8). The sign and text carry the meaning, with no colour.
  • Ions: square brackets with the overall charge outside, top right.
  • Tally: one line of Space Mono under the canvas that shows its working.
