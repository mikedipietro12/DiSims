IMF taste directions: design review page (not student-facing)

Three contrasting identities, each running the real Sandbox engine. Poke them; the auto demo stops when you interact. Same physics in all three; only the look changes. All three use model-kit (CPK) atom colours and the textbook electrostatic-map convention for partial charges: red = δ−, blue = δ+.
