Draw Lab

Pick a molecule from the list, or start with a blank canvas, and draw its Lewis structure.

  • Red number (top right): how many more electrons that atom needs for a full octet.
  • Black number (bottom right): how many of that atom’s own valence electrons we still need to place.
  • Green box: the drawing matches the accepted Lewis structure.
  • 3D: once every count is complete, click “3D” to see the shape.
