## Lewis drawing: Direction A, "Ink on the tray"
- Bold Space Grotesk ink letters for atoms, ink bond strokes (parallel lines for double and triple bonds), and ink lone-pair dots, on a plain white tray.
- **No colour while drawing Lewis structures.** No atom colours, no underlines, no gold dots. It should look like what students write on a test or the board.
- **Colour arrives with VSEPR and 3D** (Ch B·2 onward): model-kit atom hues, then δ± and lone-pair lobes in the IMF style.
- Rejected: B Vinyl chips (colour too early), C Keycap tiles (square tiles hide angles).

## Notation conventions
- **Formal charge, teaching form** (first formal-charge beats, 12–13): the full equation written out per atom with the numbers filled in.
  `FC = valence − nonbonding − ½(bonding)` → `C  FC = 4 − 2 − ½(6) = −1`
- **Formal charge, compact form** (practice beats ★14 onward, and the Lab): a small ink-outlined Space Mono pill (−1, +1), shown only when non-zero. Never red or yellow, because formal charge is bookkeeping, not a δ charge.
- **Count that is off:** a dashed ink ring around the atom plus a mono tag (6 / 8). Sign and text, no colour.
- **Ions:** square brackets with the overall charge outside, top right.
- **Tally:** one Space Mono line under the canvas that shows its working.

## Anti-patterns (additions)
- Dotted or dot-grid backgrounds (series-wide).
- Colour in Lewis structures before VSEPR.
- Red or yellow on formal-charge marks.
